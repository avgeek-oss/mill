# Code of conduct

Mill welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be considerate, make room for others, and keep technical disagreements focused on the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, disclosure of private information, and repeated disruption are not acceptable. This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community spaces managed by the maintainers.

Report concerns privately to a maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Include links or other details that help them understand what happened. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as possible and review reports fairly.

Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, or suspend participation when needed. A conflict involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another maintainer. Enforcement should explain the behavior at issue and the action taken without exposing private reports.
